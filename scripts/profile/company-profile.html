About us

Who we are

Ilelah Technologies is a technology business based in Bauchi, Nigeria. We build software, support the systems organisations depend on and help people gain the skills to use technology well.

We work with businesses, schools and organisations that need technology to work reliably in real conditions: limited budgets, busy staff, shared computers and internet connections that cannot always be trusted. We design for those conditions from the start.

We explain choices in plain language and put scope, prices and responsibilities in writing. We would rather deliver a smaller system that is used every day than a large one that is never finished.

Our mission

To help businesses, schools and organisations work better through practical, dependable technology and the skills to use it.

Our vision

To be a trusted technology partner for organisations in Nigeria and beyond, known for products and services that genuinely make work easier.

What we value

  • Practical first

    Technology that solves the problem in front of you and that you can afford to run.

  • Honest by default

    We say what we can and cannot do, quote in writing and tell you when you do not need something.

  • Built to last

    We document our work, hand over properly and design systems someone else could maintain.

  • Accessible technology

    Good software and digital skills within reach of small businesses and schools.

Our founder

Walid Bello Ilelah, Founder and CEO. His background is in Computer and Communications Engineering, and his experience spans ICT support, school ICT administration, software development, web design, digital skills training and educational technology. That mix shapes how the business works: school ICT experience informs our education technology, hands-on support keeps our software practical, and training experience means we explain technology in terms people can use.
