We build for Android because that is what most businesses, staff and customers in Nigeria use. If you need an app on other platforms as well, tell us at the start and we will discuss the options and their costs honestly.

Many of the apps we work on need to function without a reliable internet connection. We design the app around that from the start, rather than adding it later.
