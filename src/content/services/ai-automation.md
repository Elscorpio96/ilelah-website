AI can be wrong, so we design automations with checks: important answers, payments and decisions stay with a person. We tell you plainly when a simple spreadsheet or form would do the job better than AI.

Many AI services charge per use. We estimate running costs before you commit and keep your business data out of tools that would use it to train their models, wherever the service allows this.
