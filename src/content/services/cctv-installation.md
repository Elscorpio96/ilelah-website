Equipment costs depend on the number and type of cameras and how long footage needs to be kept, so every installation is quoted after a survey. You can buy equipment through us or supply your own, and we will tell you honestly if what you have is not suitable.

Cameras should be placed so they protect your property without recording places where people expect privacy. We will point out where this matters and where signs are needed.
