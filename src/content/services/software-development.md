Good software starts with a clear understanding of the job it has to do. We spend time on how your work actually happens (who records what, where mistakes creep in, which reports you need) before deciding what to build.

We keep projects as small as they can sensibly be. A focused system that your staff use every day is worth more than an ambitious one that is never finished.

Every project is priced after the requirements are understood. We do not quote fixed prices or delivery dates before that point, because any figure given earlier would be a guess.
