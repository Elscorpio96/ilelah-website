Every facility works differently, so we start with the modules that solve your biggest problem first, whether that is patient records, billing or pharmacy stock, and add more as your staff get comfortable.

Systems can run on your own local network so work continues during internet outages, or online when several branches need to share records.

Patient information is sensitive. We discuss how records are protected, who can see them and how they are backed up before anything is built, and we design with Nigeria's data protection law in mind. We do not provide medical advice or clinical decision tools.
