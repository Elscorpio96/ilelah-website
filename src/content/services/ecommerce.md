Payments go straight into your own payment provider account. We never hold your customers' money, and payment details are handled by the provider, not stored on your website.

Running an online store has costs beyond the build: payment provider fees, hosting and your time keeping products and stock up to date. We explain these clearly in the quote so there are no surprises.
